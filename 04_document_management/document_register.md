## Document Status

- **Active** — Current document that is still in use.
- **Pending** — Document awaiting review, approval, or action.
- **Archived** — Document retained for record-keeping but no longer actively used.

## Document Management Tasks

- Maintain an organized document register.
- Track document status and ownership.
- Record document locations for quick retrieval.
- Monitor updates and revisions.
- Identify documents requiring follow-up.
- Support consistent filing and archiving procedures.

## Administrative Objective

The objective of this register is to demonstrate the ability to maintain accurate, structured, and easily searchable administrative records.
