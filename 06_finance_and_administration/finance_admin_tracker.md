# Finance & Administration Support

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates practical finance and administrative support skills, including expense tracking, invoice monitoring, payment follow-up, and financial record organization.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Expense tracking
- Invoice management
- Payment follow-up
- Financial record organization
- Administrative documentation
- Data entry
- Spreadsheet management
- Record keeping
- Financial follow-up

## Main Deliverables

### Expense Tracker

A structured tool for recording and monitoring administrative and operational expenses.

### Invoice Tracker

A system for tracking invoices, payment status, due dates, and follow-up actions.

### Payment Follow-up Tracker

A tool designed to monitor outstanding payments and identify items requiring follow-up.

## Tools

- Microsoft Excel
- Microsoft Word
- GitHub

## Portfolio Objective

The objective is to demonstrate the ability to support finance and administrative operations through accurate record keeping, structured tracking systems, and timely follow-up.
